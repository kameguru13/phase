Clause-by-clause reading (CLAUDE.md "Read the card before the code"):

1. "Return target creature card from an opponent's graveyard to the battlefield": a one-shot instruction (CR 608.2c), target slot a creature card in an opponent's graveyard.
2. "under their control": "their" is the opponent whose graveyard it is, the card's owner (CR 108.3), the "states otherwise" case of CR 110.2a.
3. "with a duty counter on it": it is given one duty counter as it enters (CR 122.1, CR 122.6).
4. "For as long as that creature has a duty counter on it, …": a CR 611.2b duration. "That creature" is the permanent the card became (CR 400.7: a new object; CR 608.2c reads the anaphor against the preceding instruction). The duration ends when it has no duty counter.
5. "it is goaded": a designation (CR 701.15b) caused by the spell, so the goader is the spell's controller; it lasts for the stated duration (CR 611.2a), not CR 701.15a's default.
6. "can't attack you or a permanent you control": a CR 508.1c restriction. "You" is the spell's controller (CR 109.5, first sentence; the effect comes from a resolving spell, CR 611.2, not a static ability on the creature), fixed at resolution (CR 608.2c). "A permanent you control" is every planeswalker or battle that player controls (CR 508.1b, CR 310.5).
7. "can't block creatures you control": a CR 509.1b restriction; "creatures you control" are the spell controller's creatures, by the same latch.

### Reference Readings

- **Printed `BlockRestriction` statics "unchanged"** (Spectral Grasp "Enchanted creature can't block creatures you control."; Heat Wave "Blue creatures can't block creatures you control."): derived.
